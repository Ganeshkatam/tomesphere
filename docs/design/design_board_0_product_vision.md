# Phase 0: Product Vision

This document serves as the "north star" for every design decision made in TomeSphere. Before determining layouts, components, or colors, we must agree on what TomeSphere fundamentally is and who it serves.

## Product Identity

TomeSphere is **a modern digital study and reading environment**. 

It is not a social media feed, it is not a corporate dashboard, and it is not a flashy, gamified marketplace. It is a quiet, robust space designed for deep focus, learning, and intellectual exploration.

## Keywords & Brand Vibe

Every design choice should optimize for these qualities:
- **Calm**
- **Focused**
- **Elegant**
- **Intellectual**
- **Warm**
- **Spacious**
- **Timeless**

We explicitly **reject** aesthetics that are:
- Corporate
- Gamified
- Social-media-like
- Flashy
- Skeuomorphic

## Primary User Personas

TomeSphere serves users who treat reading as a primary pursuit:
1. **The Casual Reader**: Seeks an uninterrupted, beautiful reading experience and an easy way to discover their next book.
2. **The Student**: Needs to highlight, annotate, and organize texts into collections for studying.
3. **The Researcher**: Requires robust search, precise metadata, and cross-referencing capabilities across a large personal library.
4. **The Lifelong Learner**: Explores deep subjects and relies on high-quality curation to guide their intellectual journey.

## Core User Goals

The interface must facilitate these fundamental goals with minimal friction:
- **Discover Books**: Find high-quality, relevant content quickly without feeling overwhelmed.
- **Read Comfortably**: Enter an immersive, distraction-free reading state.
- **Organize a Personal Library**: Curate, sort, and retrieve saved books efficiently.
- **Track Reading Progress**: Understand where they are in their current books and their overall learning journey.

## Foundational Design Principles

1. **Content First**: The book (its cover, title, and text) is always the hero. The interface should recede into the background.
2. **Minimal Chrome**: Remove unnecessary borders, heavy shadows, and competing UI elements. Let typography and spacing do the structural work.
3. **Reading Without Distractions**: When in the Reader mode, all navigation and extraneous UI should disappear entirely.
4. **Progressive Disclosure**: Do not overwhelm the user with all available tools at once. Reveal complexity only when they need it.
